Radio dies on your first solo? After Friday's nationwide ground stop caught a student pilot mid-solo, here are 5 things every student should know cold.

1. Lost two-way radio: squawk 7600 (AIM 6-4-2)
2. Steady green light in the air: cleared to land (14 CFR 91.125)
3. Flashing red light in the air: airport unsafe, do not land (14 CFR 91.125)
4. Emergency frequency: 121.5 MHz, squawk 7700 for distress (AIM 6-3-1)
5. Final authority: you, the pilot in command (14 CFR 91.3)
