Can a student pilot do this? 5 situations, yes or no (save this for later)

1. Carry a passenger? No (14 CFR 61.89(a)(1))
2. Solo with 4 SM visibility at night? No, students need 3 SM by day and 5 SM at night (14 CFR 61.89(a)(6))
3. Solo on an endorsement from 4 months ago? No, it must be within 90 days (14 CFR 61.87(n))
4. Solo into Class B airspace? Only with specific training and an endorsement (14 CFR 61.95)
5. Fly solo at night? Only with night training and a night solo endorsement (14 CFR 61.87(o))
