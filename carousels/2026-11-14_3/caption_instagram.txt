5 right-of-way calls you will get asked (14 CFR 91.113)

1. Converging, same category: the aircraft on the right has the right-of-way
2. Head-on: both pilots alter course to the right
3. Glider vs airplane: the glider has the right-of-way over powered aircraft
4. Overtaking: the aircraft being overtaken has the right-of-way, pass well clear to the right
5. Two aircraft approaching to land: the lower one, but no cutting in front of someone on final

And an aircraft in distress has the right-of-way over everyone. How many did you get? Search Boplo Flight on the App Store.
