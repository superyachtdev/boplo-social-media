Told to land now. Got the fuel? This week's ground stop sent pilots looking for the nearest runway, so here are 5 fuel rules every student should know cold.

1. Day VFR in an airplane: fuel to your first point of intended landing plus 30 minutes (14 CFR 91.151(a)(1))
2. Night VFR: plus 45 minutes (14 CFR 91.151(a)(2))
3. Helicopter VFR: plus 20 minutes, day or night (14 CFR 91.151(b))
4. The reserve is figured at normal cruising speed, considering wind and forecast weather (14 CFR 91.151(a))
5. Leaving the airport area: preflight action includes weather, fuel requirements and alternatives if the flight can't be completed as planned (14 CFR 91.103(a))
