Is your medical still valid? 5 medical certificate questions examiners love to ask (save this one).

1. A private pilot needs at least a third-class medical, or BasicMed if you qualify (14 CFR 61.23(a)(3))
2. Third class, under 40 at your exam: good through the end of the 60th month after the exam month (14 CFR 61.23(d)(3)(i))
3. Third class, 40 or older at your exam: 24 calendar months (14 CFR 61.23(d)(3)(ii))
4. First-class privileges lapse? The certificate still covers private flying for 24 or 60 months depending on your age at the exam (14 CFR 61.23(d)(1))
5. A condition or medication that leaves you unable to meet the medical standards means no acting as PIC, even with a valid card (14 CFR 61.53(a))
