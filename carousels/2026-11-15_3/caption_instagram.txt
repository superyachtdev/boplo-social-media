3-152 or clear of clouds? 5 VFR weather minimums from 14 CFR 91.155 you should be able to recite on the oral.

1. Class B: 3 SM visibility, clear of clouds
2. Class C and D (and Class E below 10,000 MSL): 3 SM, 500 ft below, 1,000 ft above, 2,000 ft horizontal
3. Class G by day at or below 1,200 AGL: 1 SM, clear of clouds
4. Class E at or above 10,000 MSL: 5 SM, 1,000 ft below, 1,000 ft above, 1 SM horizontal
5. Landing at a Class D surface area under basic VFR: 3 SM ground visibility and a 1,000 ft ceiling, otherwise you need special VFR (91.155(c), (d))
