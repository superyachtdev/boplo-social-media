5 airplane facts that sound fake but are true. Most passengers never hear these.

1. Every airliner gets hit by lightning about once a year. The current travels along the outside and exits, usually without harm (FAA estimate).
2. Smoking is banned, but airliner lavatories must still have an ashtray, so anyone who lights up has a safe place to put it out (14 CFR 25.853(g)).
3. The tiny hole in your window is on purpose. It lets the outer pane carry the cabin pressure and keeps the window from fogging.
4. In Boeing's 2010 ultimate load test, a 787's wings flexed about 25 feet upward and held.
5. At airline airports, the first fire truck must reach the middle of the farthest runway within 3 minutes of the alarm (14 CFR 139.319).
