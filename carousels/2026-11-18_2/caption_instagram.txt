5 flight school costs nobody mentions. Most quotes assume the FAA minimum, but the 2026 median private pilot cost is over $16,000 (Redbird 2026 State of Flight Training survey). Save this before you sign up.

1. The hours past the minimum: the rules require 40 hours (14 CFR 61.109), but the FAA says the U.S. average is about 75. At the $180/hr median rental, 35 extra hours is about $6,300.
2. The examiner's fee: 52% of examiners charge $800 to $1,000 for a checkride, plus the airplane that day, and more if you need a retest (Redbird 2026).
3. The written test: the FAA private pilot knowledge test is $175 at PSI, every attempt.
4. A real headset: about $375 for a basic David Clark H10-13.4, up to $1,299 for a Bose A30.
5. Long gaps between lessons: the median student takes about 24 weeks. Weather cancellations and time off often mean re-flying skills you already learned.
