5 questions nervous flyers always Google. Straight answers, zero jargon.

1. Can someone open the door mid-flight? Not at cruise altitude. Cabin pressure pushes the plug-type door into its frame with tons of force.
2. Can a jet glide with no engines? Yes. In 2001, Air Transat Flight 236, an Airbus A330, ran out of fuel over the Atlantic and glided for about 19 minutes to the Azores. All 306 people survived.
3. Why are plane windows rounded? After two de Havilland Comet jets broke up in 1954, investigators traced metal fatigue to a square-cornered cutout. Curves spread the stress.
4. Can your phone crash the plane? No, but under FAA rules (14 CFR 91.21) the airline decides which devices are safe, and a phone hunting for signal can put a buzz in pilots' headsets.
5. Do both pilots eat the same meal? Often, no. Many airlines give the two pilots different meals, so one bad dish can't make both of them sick.
