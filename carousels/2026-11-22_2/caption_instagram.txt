5 things your flight bag can't miss. Forget number 1 and you can't legally fly solo.

1. Your 3 documents: student pilot certificate, medical certificate and a government photo ID (14 CFR 61.3).
2. Your logbook: on every solo cross-country it has to ride with you as proof of your instructor's endorsements (14 CFR 61.51(i)(2)).
3. Your own headset: small cockpits are loud enough to hurt your hearing over time (FAA brochure: Hearing and Noise in Aviation). Passive headsets start around $200, noise-cancelling ones run past $1,000.
4. A current sectional chart: FAA sectionals are updated every 56 days, and you need all available information before you fly (14 CFR 91.103). Paper or app, use the current edition.
5. Kneeboard, E6B and plotter: your checkride starts with a cross-country plan you build yourself (Private Pilot ACS, Area I, Task D).
