5 hidden plane features nobody points out. You've probably sat next to all of them.

1. The small triangle above some windows marks the best view of the wing, so crew can check the flaps, slats or ice from inside.
2. The yellow hooks on the wing are for escape ropes. After a water landing, crew clip a rope from the overwing exit to the hook so people can cross a wet, slippery wing.
3. Stuck aisle armrest? On many aisle seats a small button under the armrest, near the hinge, lets it fold up.
4. The lights on the floor are an escape path. Smoke rises, so airliners need exit path markings near the floor that still work when everything higher up is hidden (14 CFR 25.812(e)).
5. The crew can open a locked lavatory from outside. Every lavatory lock must open from outside without special tools, so nobody gets trapped (14 CFR 25.820). On many jets the latch hides under the "Lavatory" sign.
