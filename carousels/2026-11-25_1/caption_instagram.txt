What 5 plane announcements really mean. Busiest Thanksgiving skies in 16 years, so here's the voice upstairs, decoded.

1. "Arm doors and cross-check": the escape slides are now live. Open an armed door and a slide inflates by itself, so each crew member checks a colleague's door (14 CFR 25.810).
2. "Waiting on paperwork": it's the weight and balance sheet. No airline jet leaves without a load manifest (14 CFR 121.693). When Delta's system failed on 22 Nov, 1,000+ flights were delayed.
3. "We need to de-ice first": frost as rough as sandpaper can cut lift by as much as 30%. Orange fluid melts ice, green fluid stops new ice sticking (14 CFR 121.629, 91.527).
4. "We're in a holding pattern": racetrack laps in the sky, about 4 minutes each, with planes stacked 1,000 ft apart (AIM 5-3-8).
5. "There's a ground stop": departures are frozen at the gate when an airport or system can't take more traffic. The 13 Nov nationwide stop led to 18,000+ cancellations.
