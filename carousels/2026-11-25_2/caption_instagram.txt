5 things to know before your pilot medical. It can ground you before your first solo. Save this for later.

1. Get it before you spend big on lessons. Dual lessons need no medical, but solo flight does (14 CFR 61.23).
2. It starts online. Fill out FAA Form 8500-8 on MedXPress, then take your confirmation number to an Aviation Medical Examiner (AME) within 60 days.
3. Old diagnoses count. The form asks if you have EVER had conditions like ADHD, depression or asthma, plus doctor visits in the last 3 years. Many are fine with paperwork.
4. Leaving something out is a federal crime: fines up to $250,000, up to 5 years in prison and losing every certificate you hold (18 U.S.C. 1001, 14 CFR 67.403).
5. Have a condition? Get a consult first. Many AMEs will review records before you submit. A deferral to the FAA can take months (Special Issuance, 14 CFR 67.401).

Medical sorted? Search Boplo Flight on the App Store, the student pilot app for learning to fly.
